1. What this is

RowingTools Tracker ("the tracker") is part of rowingtools.co.uk, run by an individual in the UK. Using it means agreeing to this page. If you do not agree with it, do not create an account.

The tracker is provided as it is, for free, with no promise that it will be available, that it will keep working, or that it will still exist next season. Keep your own record of anything you would be upset to lose. You can export your exercise library from the Templates tab at any time, and you can ask for a copy of everything else.

2. Your account

You need an email address and a password. You must be 16 or over, or have a parent or guardian agree on your behalf. Give a name that your crewmates will recognise, because that is what appears to anyone in a squad with you.

Keep your password to yourself. If you think someone else has it, change it from the sign-in page. Do not use the tracker to store anything about other people without their say-so.

3. What is stored, and where

Accounts and training data are held in a Supabase project (PostgreSQL, hosted in the EU) with row-level security, so a query for your data authenticated as anyone else returns nothing. Stored against your account:

  • Your email address, display name, and when you agreed to these terms.
  • Your exercise library and core routines.
  • Every session you log: weights sets, erg pieces and splits, core rounds and times, the dates and times, and any notes you write.
  • Which squads you are in.

The site uses Google Analytics on page views, and a Brevo form if you subscribe to results emails. Neither is connected to your training log.

Sample mode

If you use the tracker without an account, everything you log is stored in your browser's local storage on that device and is never sent to us. Clearing your browser data deletes it and there is no way to get it back. Creating an account uploads what is there to your new account; that is the only time it leaves the device.

4. Squads

A squad is a group of people who can see how much training each other is getting through. Joining a squad is what puts your totals on its board. Everyone in it can see your display name and these numbers, for the window they pick (this week, four weeks, twelve weeks, all time):

  • days you trained, and how many sessions of each kind;
  • erg metres and erg time;
  • core time working;
  • weights sets and weights volume.

They cannot see an individual session, an individual lift, a date you trained, a note, or anything the erg photo reader produced. This is enforced in the database, not just in the app: the board is built by a function that never reads those columns.

Anyone with a squad's six-character code, or an invite link containing it, can join that squad and will then see the totals of everyone in it. Treat the code like a door key. The person who created a squad can remove anyone from it. Leaving a squad removes your totals from its board immediately, and removes anything you posted to it.

5. Erg photos

If you photograph an erg monitor, the image is sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which reads the numbers off it and returns them. The image is not stored by us and, under Anthropic's API terms, is not used to train their models. Only the numbers are saved, to your log. Do not photograph anything you would not want processed by a third party in this way: point the camera at the monitor, not at people.

Photo reading is a paid feature. Typing sessions in by hand is free and always unlimited.

6. Payment

Logging weights, erg and core sessions, squads, and everything else on this page is free. If a paid tier is introduced, you will be told before anything is charged and nothing you already have will be taken away from you retrospectively.

7. Your rights over your data

Under UK GDPR you can ask for a copy of everything held about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Email feedback@rowingtools.co.uk from the address on your account and it will be done. Deletion is deletion: the account and every session, exercise and squad membership attached to it are removed, and there is no archive copy.

The legal basis for holding your training data is the contract of providing you the tracker. Data is kept for as long as your account exists.
